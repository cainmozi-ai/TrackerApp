================================================================================
1. ONBOARDING (first launch)
================================================================================
- Runs once on first launch (skippable with "Skip for now").
- Collects: name, goal (Lose weight / Maintain / Gain muscle), sex, age,
  weight (kg), height (cm), activity level (5 levels from Sedentary to
  Very Active).
- Live preview card: as soon as age/weight/height are filled, the app
  calculates and shows a personalized daily plan before you commit.
- Target calculation uses the Mifflin–St Jeor equation:
  * BMR from sex/weight/height/age
  * TDEE = BMR x activity multiplier (1.2 – 1.9)
  * Goal adjustment: -500 kcal (lose), +0 (maintain), +400 (gain),
    floored at 1,200 kcal
  * Protein: 2.0 g/kg bodyweight (2.2 g/kg for muscle gain)
  * Fat: 25% of calories; Carbs: remainder
  * Water: ~35 ml/kg, converted to 250 ml glasses (clamped 6–14)
- Saves calorie / protein / carbs / fat / water targets to the profile.
- "Start Tracking" applies the plan; targets are editable later in Profile.

================================================================================
2. HOME DASHBOARD
================================================================================
- Time-aware greeting (Good Morning / Afternoon / Evening) + your name.
- Profile avatar button (top right) -> Profile/settings.
- XP / LEVEL CARD: current level number, level name (Beginner -> Apprentice ->
  Dedicated -> Committed -> Advanced -> Elite -> Master -> Champion ->
  Legend -> Mythic), total XP, and an animated progress bar to next level.
- "WORKOUT IN PROGRESS" RESUME BANNER: if an unfinished workout exists,
  a green-bordered banner appears — tap to jump straight back into it.
- BENTO STAT GRID — six live, tappable tiles (each deep-links to its module):
  * Calories eaten today vs target (with progress bar)
  * Water glasses today vs target (with progress bar)
  * Habits completed today vs total (with progress bar)
  * Sleep duration last night (e.g. "7h 30m")
  * Tasks remaining today
  * Money spent today
- QUICK-ACTION FLOATING BUTTON (+): expands to 4 shortcuts —
  Log Meal, Start Workout, Add Task, Log Water.
- All tiles animate in with a staggered entrance; cards have press feedback.

================================================================================
3. NUTRITION MODULE
================================================================================
DAILY FOOD LOG (main screen)
- Hero card: calorie ring (eaten vs target) + signed remaining display.
  Adherence-neutral philosophy: going over shows a calm "X kcal over"
  (never red, never shaming); under shows "X kcal left".
- Three macro progress bars: Protein, Carbs, Fat (current g / target g).
- Four meal cards: Breakfast, Lunch, Dinner, Snack — each shows its foods,
  per-item calories and servings, a per-meal calorie subtotal, a one-tap
  "+" that opens the food picker pre-set to that meal, and per-item delete.
- COPY YESTERDAY: one tap re-logs everything you ate yesterday into today.
- SAVE AS MEAL: saves the entire current day's foods as a named, reusable
  combo meal (e.g. "My usual breakfast").

ADD FOOD (picker screen) — 4 logging methods
1) SEARCH: queries your local food database PLUS the Open Food Facts online
   database (1M+ products) — results show calories, protein/carbs/fat and
   serving size; one tap to log.
2) BARCODE SCANNER: live camera scan (EAN-13/EAN-8/UPC-A/UPC-E) -> instant
   lookup on Open Food Facts -> nutrition card -> pick meal -> log.
   Graceful "product not found" fallback with manual-entry option.
3) AI PHOTO RECOGNITION: photograph your meal; Google Gemini vision
   estimates each food with calories + macros; review the detected list,
   pick a meal slot, and log all items at once. (Requires a free
   EXPO_PUBLIC_GEMINI_API_KEY in .env; without one the screen shows
   friendly setup instructions and offers Search/Scan instead.)
4) CUSTOM FOOD: full manual entry — name, brand, serving size/unit,
   calories, protein, carbs, fat, fiber, sugar.

FAST LOGGING (default view before you type anything)
- RECENT tab: your 20 most-recently-logged foods, one tap to re-log.
- FAVORITES tab: starred foods for instant logging.
- MEALS tab: your saved combo meals with total calories — one tap logs the
  whole combo (multiple foods at once).
- Meal-slot chips (Breakfast/Lunch/Dinner/Snack) selectable at the top.

================================================================================
4. WATER TRACKING
================================================================================
- Large circular progress ring: glasses today vs daily target, plus
  ml total (e.g. "1250ml / 2000ml").
- Quick-add buttons: +1 Glass, +250ml, +500ml, +1L.
- Today's log list with timestamps; individual entries deletable.
- XP for logging; bonus XP + achievement the moment you hit your daily goal.

================================================================================
5. SLEEP TRACKING
================================================================================
- Log bedtime + wake time (handles overnight crossing midnight); duration
  auto-calculated (e.g. "7h 45m").
- Quality rating 1–5 stars (Terrible -> Great) + optional notes.
- One entry per day (re-logging the same day updates it).
- Today card showing last night's sleep at a glance.
- Recent history list with date, duration, and star ratings.

================================================================================
6. WEIGHT TRACKING + TREND
================================================================================
- Quick daily weigh-in entry (one entry per day; same-day re-log updates).
- SMOOTHED TREND LINE: an exponentially-weighted moving average cuts
  through daily water/food weight noise (MacroFactor-style).
- Chart overlays raw scale dots (faded) with the smoothed trend curve
  (green) over the last 60 days.
- Stat row: current trend weight, total change over the window, log count.
- Recent log list with delete.

================================================================================
7. ADAPTIVE COACH (the standout feature)
================================================================================
- Estimates your REAL maintenance calories (expenditure/TDEE) from your own
  data using energy balance:
  TDEE ~= average daily intake − (change in weight trend x 7700 kcal/kg ÷ days)
- Requires ~1 week of food logs + at least 2 weight entries spanning 6+ days;
  until then it shows a friendly "keep logging to unlock your coach" state.
- Expenditure card: estimated kcal/day maintenance, your weekly weight-trend
  rate (e.g. "-0.4 kg/week"), and how many days of data it's based on.
- RECOMMENDED TARGET card: recalculates calories + full macro split from
  your measured expenditure and goal; shows your current target alongside.
- One-tap "Apply recommended target" updates all profile targets.
- Always a suggestion, never forced — recalibrates continuously as you log.

================================================================================
8. MEAL PLANNER + SHOPPING LIST
================================================================================
- Weekly grid: 7 days x 4 meal slots; navigate between weeks.
- Tap any slot to add foods from your database (search picker).
- Planned items shown as removable chips per slot.
- GENERATE SHOPPING LIST: aggregates every planned food for the week into a
  checklist with total servings per item.
- Shopping list screen: tap-to-check items (strikethrough), progress count
  ("4 of 11 items"), clear-all.

================================================================================
9. WORKOUTS MODULE
================================================================================
EXERCISE LIBRARY
- 90+ pre-loaded exercises across Chest, Back, Shoulders, Legs, Glutes,
  Arms, Core, Cardio — each with equipment type and description.
- Search + muscle-group filter chips.
- Add your own custom exercises (name + muscle group).

PROGRAMS (leveled program library)
- Browsable library of complete multi-day training programs, filterable by
  experience level: Beginner / Intermediate / Advanced.
- Included programs (original, principle-based):
  * Foundations — Beginner, 3 days/week, Full Body (A/B/C days)
  * Minimalist — Beginner, 2 days/week, Full Body
  * Upper/Lower Builder — Intermediate, 4 days/week (Upper A/B, Lower A/B)
  * Push/Pull/Legs — Advanced, 6 days/week (Push/Pull/Legs A+B)
- Each program card shows level, days/week, split, its workout days, and
  the muscle groups it covers.
- "Add to My Routines" clones every day of the program into your own
  editable routines.

MY ROUTINES (custom templates)
- Create named routines; add exercises from the library with target sets
  and a REP RANGE (min–max); reorder/remove; delete routines.
- Start a workout directly from any routine.
- "Start Empty Workout" for freestyle sessions.

ACTIVE WORKOUT (live session)
- Live stats bar: exercise count, total sets, total volume (kg).
- REST TIMER: auto-starts the moment you log a set; +/- 15s adjust,
  pause/reset, quick presets (60s / 90s / 2m / 3m), "Rest complete!" state.
- Per-exercise cards show:
  * Muscle group + target rep range (e.g. "8–12 reps")
  * "Last: 60x8, 60x8, 60x7" — your previous session inline
  * PROGRESSION SUGGESTION chip (rule-based, MacroFactor-style):
    if you hit the top of your rep range on all sets last time, it suggests
    +2.5 kg (+1.25 kg for lighter lifts) at the bottom of the range;
    otherwise same weight, +1 rep. Tap the chip to prefill.
  * Logged sets with a color dot per set type
- CUSTOM SET KEYPAD (no fiddly OS keyboard): big weight/reps fields, digit
  pad, backspace, decimal; SET TYPE chips (Normal / Warm-up / Failure /
  Drop); optional RIR (reps-in-reserve 0–4); large green confirm logs the
  set and auto-starts the rest timer.
- PLATE CALCULATOR: enter total bar weight, choose bar (20/15/10 kg), get
  the exact plates per side (25/20/15/10/5/2.5/1.25), with a note for any
  remainder that can't be plated.
- Add extra exercises mid-workout from a searchable picker.
- Finish Workout -> +50 XP and the session is saved to history.

WORKOUT CONTINUITY (never lose a session)
- Backing out or even killing the app does NOT lose your workout — it stays
  "in progress" in the database.
- Resume banners on the Fitness tab and Home dashboard reconnect you with
  every set intact.
- The workout row is only created when you log your FIRST set (no empty
  orphan workouts).
- Starting a new workout while one is unfinished prompts:
  Resume / Discard & start new.
- Explicit Discard action (trash icon, with confirmation) inside the session.

PROGRESS
- Total workouts completed counter.
- SET LEVELS: weekly sets-per-muscle-group volume bars (training-volume
  distribution at a glance).
- Per-exercise strength chart: pick any exercise -> max-weight trend line
  across your last sessions.
- Recent workout history list with dates.

11. HABITS
================================================================================

  (it pops with a spring animation and fills green with a checkmark);
  long-press opens detail/stats.
- Per-habit fire-icon STREAK counter (consecutive days).

  and a GitHub-style 90-day HEATMAP calendar.
- Habits support daily or x-times-per-week frequency.

16. DESIGN SYSTEM
================================================================================
- MacroFactor-inspired aesthetic: charcoal surfaces (#121212/#1C1C1E),
  near-white text, muted grayscale module tones, ONE green accent
  (#34D27A) carrying all interactive/data emphasis.
- Refined light theme available via the toggle.
- Consistent component kit: screen headers, motion cards (press-scale +
  staggered fade-in entrances), warm empty states with calls-to-action,
  bento stat tiles, progress rings, category icon picker, speed-dial FAB.
- Restrained spring motion; denser shape scale; data-first hierarchy.
- Friendly, non-judgmental microcopy throughout (reward, never punish).

================================================================================
17. DATA & PRIVACY
================================================================================
- Everything stored locally in SQLite on your device — no account, no
  sign-up, no data leaves the phone (except food searches to Open Food
  Facts and optional AI photo calls to Gemini).
- 20+ tables: profile, foods, food logs, saved meals, meal plans, shopping
  list, water, sleep, weight, exercises, templates/programs, workout logs
  & sets, tasks, habits + logs, transactions, categories, achievements,
  XP log, events.
- Idempotent migrations keep existing installs upgrading safely.
- NOTE: the web demo uses an in-memory database (resets on page refresh);
  the installed Android app persists everything.

18. PLATFORMS & DEPLOYMENT
================================================================================
- ANDROID: EAS cloud build configured (eas.json) — "preview" profile
  produces an installable APK; "production" profile builds an AAB for the
  Play Store. Build with: eas build -p android --profile preview
- WEB: single-page Expo web export, deployed on Netlify with the COOP/COEP
  headers required for SQLite WebAssembly and SPA routing fallback.
